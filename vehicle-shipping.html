Ship Your Car Across the U.S.

Need your vehicle moved to another state? Auto transport services pick up your car, truck, or SUV and deliver it door-to-door anywhere in the country.

How Vehicle Shipping Works

Auto transport is the industry term for moving a vehicle from one location to another without driving it yourself. Carriers load your vehicle onto an open or enclosed trailer and deliver it to your destination. The process is straightforward:

  1. Get a quote. Enter your pickup and delivery locations, vehicle type, and preferred dates. Most companies provide an instant estimate.
  2. Book your shipment. Once you accept a quote, the carrier assigns a pickup window. Most pickups happen within 3 to 7 days of booking.
  3. Vehicle pickup. A driver inspects your vehicle, documents its condition, and loads it onto the transport trailer.
  4. Transit. Cross-country shipments typically take 7 to 14 days depending on distance and route.
  5. Delivery. The carrier delivers your vehicle to the destination. You inspect it again and sign off on the condition report.

When Does It Make Sense to Ship?

Relocating to a New State

Moving across the country for work or family? Shipping your car saves you a multi-day drive and the wear on your vehicle. Fly to your new city and have your car meet you there.

Buying a Car Out of State

Found a better deal on a car three states away? Auto transport lets you buy from any dealer or private seller in the country without making the trip yourself.

Seasonal Moves

Snowbirds heading south for winter or returning north in spring often ship one vehicle rather than driving both. It is faster, easier, and avoids highway miles.

Military PCS Moves

Service members on permanent change of station orders frequently use auto transport. Many carriers offer military discounts and understand the timeline pressure of PCS moves.

What Does It Cost?

Vehicle shipping costs depend on distance, vehicle size, transport type (open vs. enclosed), and time of year. Here are typical ranges:

Route Distance Typical Cost
Within the same region Under 500 miles $400 - $700
Cross-country (e.g. CA to FL) 2,000 - 3,000 miles $900 - $1,400
Coast to coast (e.g. NY to LA) 2,500 - 3,000 miles $1,000 - $1,600
Enclosed transport (any distance) Varies +30% - 50% premium

Prices vary by season. Summer and January are the busiest months and tend to cost more. Flexible pickup dates can save you money.

Open vs. Enclosed Transport

Open transport is the standard option. Your vehicle rides on an open-air trailer alongside other cars. It is the most affordable choice and what most people use. About 90% of vehicles are shipped this way.

Enclosed transport uses a fully covered trailer that protects your vehicle from weather and road debris. It costs more but is worth it for luxury cars, classic vehicles, or anything you want extra protection for.

Tips Before You Ship

  • Remove personal belongings from the vehicle. Carriers are not liable for items left inside.
  • Note any existing damage before pickup and take photos for your records.
  • Make sure the vehicle starts and drives. Non-running vehicles cost extra to load.
  • Keep the gas tank at about a quarter full to reduce weight.
  • Disable toll transponders and parking passes to avoid charges during transit.
  • Ask about insurance coverage. Reputable carriers include cargo insurance, but verify the details.
