Frequently Asked Questions

What is Founding Member pricing?

The first 50 businesses to subscribe to a Featured plan get their rate locked in permanently. Your monthly rate stays $29/month (or $199/year) for life, even if prices increase later. This pricing is protected as long as your subscription remains active.

What happens if I cancel my Founding Member subscription?

If you cancel, your grandfathered Founding Member pricing is permanently lost. If you resubscribe later, you will pay the current rate at that time.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. You can upgrade from monthly to annual at any time to save $149 per year. Contact us to make the switch and we will adjust your billing.

How does the Featured badge work?

Featured listings display a yellow "Featured" badge and are shown above all free listings on your city page and in search results. Your listing card also gets a highlighted border to stand out visually.

Is the free listing really free forever?

Yes. Free listings include your business name, phone, website, address, and description with no expiration date and no hidden fees. You can upgrade to Featured at any time.

How do I manage my subscription?

After subscribing, you will receive an email with a link to manage your billing, update payment information, change plans, or cancel. You can also contact us at info@knowleapstrategies.com for help.
