Privacy Policy

How TowTruck.blog handles your information.

Information We Collect

TowTruck.blog is a static directory website. We collect information only when you voluntarily submit it through our listing submission form (business name, phone, email, services). We do not collect personal data from visitors who are simply browsing the directory.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our forms is used to create and manage business listings on the directory. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand visitor traffic and improve the directory. Analytics may collect anonymous data such as pages visited, time on site, and general location. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels beyond basic analytics.

Click-to-Call

When you tap a phone number on a listing, your device initiates a phone call directly to the towing company. We may track click-to-call events anonymously through analytics to measure directory usage. We do not record or intercept calls.

Third-Party Links

Listings may link to towing company websites, review platforms (Google, Yelp), and affiliate partners (Amazon). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on TowTruck.blog are affiliate links. If you purchase a product through an affiliate link, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. This does not influence which businesses are listed in our directory.

Data Security

TowTruck.blog is hosted on GitHub Pages and served over HTTPS. We take reasonable precautions to protect submitted information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
