Towing in Hilo

Hilo is the largest city on Hawaii's Big Island, located on the eastern coast. The area's tropical climate brings frequent rain, which can make roads slippery and increase the need for roadside assistance. From the winding roads near Hawaii Volcanoes National Park to the busy streets of downtown Hilo, local towing companies are familiar with the terrain.

If you need a tow in Hilo, call ahead for availability. Big Island towing companies typically offer flatbed towing, roadside assistance, accident recovery, and long-distance hauling between Hilo and Kona.
