About Tadio's Towing

Tadio's Towing started with a single truck and a commitment to showing up when Honolulu drivers needed help. Over the past decade, the business has grown through word-of-mouth referrals and repeat customers who know they can count on a fast, reliable response at any hour.

Locally owned LLC based in Honolulu, HI 96816. Every call is answered by someone who knows the island's roads, traffic patterns, and neighborhoods. That local knowledge translates into faster arrival times and smoother service.

Services

Towing

Light-duty towing, car towing, local and long-distance hauling. Flatbed and wheel-lift options for safe transport.

Roadside Assistance

Flat tire changes, jump starts, battery service, and auto lockouts. Quick response to get you back on the road.

Recovery & Specialty

Winch-outs, accident recovery, special vehicle towing, and junk car removal. Free junk car removal with same-day pickup available.
