Towing in Hawaii

Hawaii's unique geography means towing companies need to know island roads, steep grades, and remote areas. Whether you are stuck on the H-1 freeway in Honolulu, dealing with a breakdown on the North Shore, or need a vehicle transported across the Big Island, local tow truck companies understand the terrain and can respond accordingly.

Most towing companies in Hawaii offer 24-hour emergency service, flatbed towing, roadside assistance (jump starts, lockouts, tire changes), accident recovery, and junk car removal. Prices vary by island and distance, so always confirm a quote before authorizing service.
