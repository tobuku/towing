Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using TowTruck.blog and getting your business listed.

For Drivers

How do I find a tow truck near me?

Use the search bar on our homepage or browse by state. Select your state, then your city, and you will see a list of towing companies with ratings, hours, and phone numbers.

Is this service free for drivers?

Yes. TowTruck.blog is completely free for drivers. Browse listings, compare companies, and call directly at no cost.

How do I call a towing company from the listing?

Every listing has a click-to-call phone number. On mobile, tap the number and your phone will dial it directly. On desktop, the number is displayed for you to call manually.

Are these real companies?

Yes. All listings are sourced from public business directories and verified where possible. We display real ratings from Google and Yelp, along with actual business addresses and phone numbers.

What should I do while waiting for a tow truck?

Stay in a safe location away from traffic. Turn on your hazard lights. If you are on a highway, stay behind a guardrail if possible. Let the towing company know about any safety concerns when you call.

How much does a tow cost?

Towing costs vary by location, distance, vehicle type, and time of day. Most companies charge a base fee plus a per-mile rate. Call the company directly for a quote before authorizing the tow.

For Towing Companies

How do I get my business listed?

Fill out the form on our List Your Business page. We will add your company to the appropriate city page with your name, phone, hours, and services.

Is it free to list my business?

Basic listings are free. Featured listings with top placement and a dedicated page are available for a fee. Contact us for pricing.

What is a featured listing?

Featured listings appear at the top of city pages with a highlighted badge. They also get a dedicated listing page with extended details, reviews, and service descriptions. Featured listings drive more calls.

How do I update my listing information?

Submit the form on our contact page with the updated information and we will make the changes.
